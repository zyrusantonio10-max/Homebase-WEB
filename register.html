      What Homebase collects and why

      Only what a dormitory needs to give you a room.

      Name, email, password
      To sign you in. Your password is stored scrambled, not as you typed it.
      Date of birth
      To know whether you are under 18. If you are, a parent or guardian must approve your account. You cannot change it later.
      Mobile number, school or occupation, student ID (if student), home province
      So the dormitory office can reach you and know who is staying in the rooms. The province is optional.
      Emergency contact, or parent or guardian
      Only used in an emergency, or to approve the account of a resident under 18.
      Who can see it
      The dormitory office sees your details and requests. Other residents can see the name, photo and course or profession of adult residents staying in a room they look at. Residents under 18 are never shown to other residents.
      Your choices
      You can correct your details on your Profile page, and delete your account there. Deleting removes your requests and reviews too.
      Where it is kept
      In this version, only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server yet.
