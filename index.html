How matching works

You tell Homebase what you need. It compares each room to that and ranks rooms by how well they fit you.

Budget
Your monthly limit, compared with what the room really costs each month.
Commute
How you get to school and how far the room is from it.
Amenities
The things you say you need in a room.
Safety
The safety needs you set for the area around the room.
