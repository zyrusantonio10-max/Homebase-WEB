The Problem

The cheapest room isn't necessarily the best option.

Students who rent a room in a dormitory often choose based on one thing: "It's cheap." Inside the same dormitory, a ₱4,500 room could mean ₱900 electricity, ₱250 water, and ₱400 for Wi-Fi — bringing your true bill to ₱6,050/month for a shared room with no study area.

Meanwhile, a ₱5,500 room with utilities and Wi-Fi included, aircon, and a private bathroom is actually ₱550 cheaper per month overall.

Homebase asks two essential questions:
  • Which room in Ariate Dormitory is actually best for THIS particular student?
  • How can the dormitory manager keep rooms, rates, and tenants up to date without paper records?

Decision Support Workflow

Personalized Guidance in 3 Clear Steps

More than a static listing directory — Homebase evaluates every room in Ariate Dormitory against your unique criteria.

Step 01

1. Set Your Preferences

Specify your monthly budget (₱4,000–₱7,000), room type (solo, duo, shared), bathroom needs, cooling (aircon vs fan), and roommate threshold.

Step 02

2. Weighted Criteria Scoring

Adjust your priority weights (Affordability 30%, Room Fit 20%, Amenities 15%, Quietness 10%, Internet 10%, Reviews 10%, Safety 5%). The algorithm computes a 0–100% suitability score for every available room.

Step 03

3. True Cost & Transparency

Review transparent itemized bills (rent + electricity + water + internet), expandable "Why This Room?" explanations, and reserve directly with the Ariate Dormitory manager.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Homebase's decision-support system and Ariate Dormitory.

Ordinary rental websites only display the base rent. Homebase calculates True Monthly Cost = Base Rent + Electricity + Water + Internet + Recurring Fees. If a room at Ariate Dormitory includes utilities in the base rent, those add-on values count as ₱0. This ensures students can accurately evaluate real monthly expenditures.

Suitability is calculated dynamically across 7 customizable criteria: Affordability, Room Fit, Amenities, Quiet Environment, Internet Quality, Reviews, and Safety. Your priorities automatically scale to 100%, and each room receives a normalized 0–100% score tailored to your personal requirements.

Homebase solves the decision problem for a specific community (Ariate Dormitory in Naga City), comparing rooms within the building rather than multiple competing facilities. All data is structured with clean identifiers so another dormitory can adopt the system simply by loading its own room data without code modifications.

The manager logs in to add, edit, or disable rooms, set monthly rates, configure house rules, review tenant reservations (Approve, Move In, Move Out, Cancel), and moderate student reviews. Any change updates the frontend immediately.
