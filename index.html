What We Analyze

Every factor that makes or breaks a place

We don't just list properties — we understand what makes them right for you.

Rent vs. Real Budget

We compare rent against your actual budget — including hidden utilities and deposits — not just the listing price.

Commute Analysis

Know the exact walk, jeep, or trike time to campus — calculated from real routes, not straight-line distance.

Amenity Matching

Wi-Fi, laundry, CCTV, curfew rules — we check what each place actually offers against your must-haves.

Utility Breakdown

Electricity, water, internet — we estimate true monthly costs so you never get blindsided by bills.

Safety & Environment

Flood risk, neighborhood safety indicators, and security features — so you can feel at home.

Smart Match Score
