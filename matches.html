How the score works
  • Four parts, 100 points. Budget, amenities, room type and floor.
  • Balanced (the default) is budget 40, amenities 30, room type 15 and floor 15. If you pick a top priority, that part counts about 45 points and the others share the rest.
  • Budget. A room at 90% of your limit or less earns the full budget points. A room closer to your limit earns about 60%, because there is little cushion. A room over your limit earns none, and is shown in its own section if it costs up to 20% more.
  • Amenities. Shared out by how many of your chosen amenities the room has. If you pick none, every room gets full points.
  • Room type and floor. Full points if the room matches, or if you chose Any.
  • Labels. Great fit means 85 or more with nothing missing, the right room type and floor, and within budget. Otherwise 65 or more is a Good fit, 45 to 64 Okay, and below 45 Weak.
  • Rooms with no free bed are never listed. A shared room (Double or Quad) stays listed until its last bed is taken.
