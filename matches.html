How the score works
  • Budget, 40 points. Only rooms at or under your limit are listed. A room at 90% of your limit or less gets all 40. A room closer to your limit gets 25, because there is little cushion.
  • Amenities, 30 points. Shared out by how many of your chosen amenities the room has. If you pick none, every room gets 30.
  • Room type, 15 points. All 15 if the type matches, or if you chose Any.
  • Floor, 15 points. All 15 if the room is on your preferred floor, or if you have no preference.
  • Labels. Great fit means 85 or more with nothing missing and the right room type and floor. Otherwise 65 or more is a Good fit, 45 to 64 Okay, and below 45 Weak.
  • Rooms that are taken or on hold are never listed.
